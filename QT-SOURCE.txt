RhineSign uses unmodified Qt / PySide6 6.10.0 shared libraries.
The libraries may be replaced by compatible builds; their license texts are in Qt-Licenses.
The application's own source and the patched signing library are in CorrespondingSource.zip.

Qt for Python, including Shiboken and PySide source:
https://download.qt.io/official_releases/QtForPython/pyside6/PySide6-6.10.0-src/
Qt source modules, release 6.10.0:
https://download.qt.io/archive/qt/6.10/6.10.0/submodules/

The matching unmodified source archives are supplied alongside the binary release.
Qt source archives retain their original licenses.
